[TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS]
Duration: 15 seconds. Aspect ratio 9:16 vertical. Single continuous handheld smartphone take — no cuts, no transitions, no hidden edits. Raw amateur iPhone capture: auto-exposure, auto-focus, auto white balance, all visibly hunting and failing in real time. No color grading, no LUT, no styled lighting, no stabilization rig. 30fps native motion feel with rolling-shutter skew on fast pans and jello-wobble on impacts. Photorealistic. Diegetic sound only — absolutely no music, no score, no sound bed.

[CORE CONCEPT]
A bystander films a matte black low-slung mid-engine V10 supercar rolling slowly down a sun-bleached Los Angeles street. The window is down. Someone on the sidewalk shouts a question at the driver. The driver answers with total calm — and then, one beat later, dumps full throttle and disappears, leaving the operator physically unable to keep up. The humor and the flex come from the contrast between the mundane answer and the violence of the departure.

BYSTANDER — young individual, casual streetwear, appears only at the left edge of frame, mostly back-of-head and shoulder, face never fully resolved. Locked wardrobe and build.

[ENVIRONMENT]
Wide Los Angeles street, mid-afternoon, harsh overhead sun almost directly above. Pale grey asphalt with heat shimmer, faded lane paint, oil stains, cracked patch repairs. Sidewalk with concrete seams and scattered litter. Chain-link fence roughly twenty feet behind the car, low commercial buildings, utility poles and sagging power lines, a few dusty palms. Parked cars along the far curb. Loose crowd of pedestrians. Air is dry and hazy. Environment stays geometrically consistent the entire take — buildings, fence, parked cars and pole positions never morph, shift or regenerate as the camera pans.

[CAMERA]
Handheld vertical phone held at chest-to-eye height by an untrained operator reacting live, not composing. Wide phone lens with mild barrel distortion and edge softness. Framing is imperfect throughout: heads clipped, subjects off-center, tilt never fully level. Micro-shake constant but not seizure-like. Focus hunts aggressively and repeatedly, locking onto the wrong plane before snapping back. Auto-exposure visibly lurches when the frame moves between sun and shade. Chromatic aberration bleeds purple and green along high-contrast edges. All reframing is physically motivated by the operator chasing the car — never a cut, never a perspective jump.
